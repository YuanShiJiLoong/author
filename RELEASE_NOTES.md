## v1.2.64 — 新增快捷词，打出词的开头按 Tab 补全

### 中文

#### 桌面端与网页版

- **快捷词**：写正文时打出词的开头，比如人名的第一个字，光标下方会弹出候选。按 Tab 填入，紧接着再按 Tab 换成下一个，按 Esc 收起。方向键和回车照常使用，用输入法打拼音的过程中不会弹出。
- **设定集里的名字自动可用**：人物、地点、物品等条目的名字会直接出现在候选里，"名字（别称）""甲/乙"这类写法会拆成两个词。
- **从书稿里找词**：点工具栏右侧的「快捷词」，AI 会读设定集、大纲和最近几章，挑出人名、地名、招式这类常打的词，你确认后再加入。也可以自己加词、删词，不提示某个名字，或者整体关掉提示。
- 快捷词按作品分别保存在本机，不会上传到云端。
- 作品打不开时的各种提示改成了更好懂的说法；桌面端崩溃提示先说明该怎么做，原因和日志位置放在最后。
- 帮助页和 README 补充了快捷词的说明。

#### 安卓端

- 本次没有功能变化，安装包版本号同步更新。

### English

#### Desktop and Web

- **Quick words**: while writing, type the start of a word, such as the first characters of a name, and suggestions pop up under the cursor. Press Tab to insert one, press Tab again right away to switch to the next, and press Esc to close. Arrow keys and Enter work as usual, and nothing pops up while you are still composing with an input method.
- **Names from your settings work right away**: names of characters, places, items and other entries appear as suggestions automatically; names written as "Name (alias)" or "A/B" are split into separate words.
- **Find words in your manuscript**: open **Quick words** on the right of the toolbar and AI reads your settings, outline and recent chapters, then picks names, places, skills and other words you type often; they are added after you confirm. You can also add or remove words yourself, stop suggesting a particular name, or turn suggestions off.
- Quick words are saved per work on this device and are not uploaded to the cloud.
- Messages shown when a work can't be opened are now easier to understand; the desktop crash dialog explains what to do first and lists the cause and log location last.
- The Help page and README now describe quick words.

#### Android

- No feature changes in this release; the app version number is updated to match.
